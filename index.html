About John

John Nuzzi is a competitive dancer and emerging choreographer based in Central Florida. Trained across five seasons at Celebration Arts Academy under director and choreographer Dixon Van Hoozer-Bowles, John has built a versatile performance vocabulary spanning contemporary, lyrical, jazz, and hip-hop disciplines. His command of musicality and spatial awareness on stage earned him some of the most coveted titles in the regional competition circuit, including the TITANIUM level award and Grand Champion honors across multiple disciplines and competitions.

Over the course of his training, John evolved well beyond the role of student. He has taken on choreographic work and served as a teaching assistant, guiding younger dancers with the same precision and artistry that defined his own competitive career. That growth was formally recognized at Celebration Arts Academy with the 5-Year Legacy Award — "The Architect" — honoring dancers who leave a defining mark on the program. John brings that same standard of commitment to every room he enters.
